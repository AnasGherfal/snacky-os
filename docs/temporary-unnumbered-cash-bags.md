# Temporary cash handling: unnumbered physical bags (2026-11-06)

Until Snacky receives uniquely numbered tamper-evident cash bags, the operator must **not invent a physical bag or seal number**.

## Machine pickup

- Choose **كيس بدون رقم** (the temporary default) in the machine's cash step.
- The authenticated server creates a stable **electronic reference** from the original pickup submission ID. It is unique and idempotent, but **is not written on or attached to the physical bag**.
- Photograph the closed bag at the machine. Record compartments and any incomplete removal honestly. Do not enter an expected or counted amount at pickup.
- The original machine, route, operator, timestamp and photo remain attached to the collection. Keep physical bags separate until safe deposit and make sure the photo shows distinguishing features. Without identification there is a mixing risk.

## Drop-off at the safe

- In **إيداع الأكياس**, select the machine's existing pickup by machine name and time. **Do not type or retype a bag number.**
- Photograph the closed bag beside the safe opening, make the actual deposit, and submit.
- The system checks the selected collection, its current custody stage, operator and revision, and preserves the photo/idempotent request.
- The recorded seal condition is **mismatch**, **not intact**, because there is no physical printed seal to verify. The owner must compare photos and records. A deposit claim is not independent receipt or physical counting.
- An older manually typed record (for example a machine-name mnemonic) may also be processed as **unnumbered**, without modifying the original historical reference; the deposit explicitly records the verification exception.

## Independent receiving and counting

- An authorized receiver physically opens the safe and checks pickup machine/time and pickup/drop-off photos before acknowledging receipt.
- Use **استلام وعدّ الأكياس** to mark unnumbered items as a seal exception, never verified physical serials.
- Count the real amount in person. Financial posting happens from the independently counted amount, once; XY reconciliation is separate.
- The owner-facing cash audit must retain the unverified-seal status. Existing physical cash-close requirements stay strict; unnumbered cash is not silently classified as a verified numbered-bag custody chain.

## When numbered bags arrive

Switch each pickup to **كيس أمني مرقّم**. The printed code is then required at pickup, drop-off and physical receipt as before. Old internal references remain in historical records and cannot be turned into genuine printed seals retrospectively.
